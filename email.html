RoboSoccer 5.0

The arena is back — and it's louder than ever. RoboSoccer 5.0 is your chance to design, build, and battle your very own custom soccer robot in a high-energy three-day showdown. Choose your fight in the Lightweight or Heavyweight category and take on competitors from across the IITM BS community.

Never built a robot before? No problem — workshops are available to get you started. All you need is a passion for robotics and the drive to get your hands dirty.

We are incredibly excited to see the innovation and competitive spirit you all bring to the arena. Don't miss out on the chance to be part of something truly electrifying!
